# Yimir: Project Overview & Vision

## Project Summary

Yimir is an intelligent knowledge base template that combines structured data management with AI-powered automation. Originally designed as a universal HR framework, the project has evolved into a sophisticated system for automating the creation of personalized interview kits and maintaining live documentation that automatically reflects the current state of your project.

## Vision & Goals

Yimir's vision is to become the central nervous system for organizational knowledge, enabling seamless access and intelligent utilization of information across all domains. Our primary goals include:

*   **Automate Knowledge Management**: Reduce manual effort in maintaining and updating organizational knowledge.
*   **Enhance Information Accessibility**: Provide intuitive and AI-powered access to critical information for both humans and agents.
*   **Ensure Data Integrity**: Maintain the accuracy and consistency of all stored knowledge through automated validation.
*   **Facilitate Live Documentation**: Ensure that project documentation is always up-to-date and reflects the current state of development.
*   **Support Multi-Domain Context**: Scale to manage knowledge across various organizational domains (HR, Finance, etc.).

## Key Stakeholders

Yimir primarily benefits:

*   **Developers**: By providing live documentation and structured project context.
*   **HR Professionals**: Through automated interview kit generation and organized HR knowledge.
*   **Project Managers**: By offering high-level project dashboards and insights.
*   **AI Agents**: By providing a structured and accessible knowledge base for intelligent operations.
